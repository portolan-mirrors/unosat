**UNOSAT code: VO20211025VUT**  This map illustrates the volcanic ashfall dispersion as observed using Sentinel-2 imagery acquired on  23 October 2021, 10:00 hrs. local time over Yasur volcano on Tanna Island, Vanuatu. At the time of the acquisition, the ashfall covered about 20 km2 at the Southwestern direction of the volcanic crater. Using the building footprints of Humanitarian Open Street Map as a baseline, around 440 buildings in the cloud free zone are located within areas covered by ash and smoke, representing 3% of the total number of buildings in Tanna island.
